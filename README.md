## The Zion System™ — What Makes It Different

Most "AI for kids" products are generic chatbots with a kid-friendly skin. They improvise. They go off-topic. They get inconsistent.

**The Zion System™ is none of those things.**

It's 10 specialized AI tutors, each built for a single subject, each calibrated for your child's exact age and level. Not one AI wearing different hats — ten distinct engines, each one expert in its domain.

The mechanism that makes it work:

1. **Specialization over generality** — Each tutor knows its subject deeply. Math Lab runs a structured 50-problem session. It doesn't wander into spelling.
2. **Session structure, not conversation** — Every tutor runs a defined session with a clear beginning, middle, and end. Predictability builds habit.
3. **Real-child calibration** — Every tutor was tested and refined on Zion. What's in this system is what actually worked.
4. **Parent-free operation** — After the first setup, your child runs the session themselves. The tutor asks their name and age, adapts, and starts.
5. **Session memory** — Each tutor outputs a summary at the end of every session. Paste it in next time and it picks up exactly where it left off.
6. **Community accountability** — The Skool community has parents running the same system, sharing what works, tracking results together.

### Setting Up Your Tutors (10 Minutes, One Time)

> You have two options — ChatGPT (Option A) or Claude (Option B). Both work. Pick whichever your family already uses.

#### Option A: ChatGPT

1. Click any tutor link above — opens directly in ChatGPT
2. Click **"Add to My GPTs"** — saves to your sidebar permanently
3. Open it with your child — it asks their name and age, then starts the lesson
4. Repeat for each tutor you want

No configuration needed. The tutor adapts to your child's name, age, and level automatically.

#### Option B: Claude Projects (claude.ai)

Claude Projects let you save each tutor with your child's info already loaded — no re-entering name and age every session.

1. Go to **[claude.ai](https://claude.ai)** → click **"Projects"** in the left sidebar
2. Click **"New Project"** → name it after the tutor (e.g., "Math Lab")
3. Click **"Project Instructions"** → paste the tutor prompt from the link in the table above
4. In **"Project Knowledge"**, add: `My child's name is [NAME], age [AGE].`
5. Open the project and start — the tutor already knows your child

Repeat for each tutor. 2 minutes per tutor.

> **Claude vs ChatGPT:** Claude Projects remember your child's info across every session. ChatGPT Custom GPTs ask for it on first run. Either works — Claude Projects just skip the intro step each time.

### Session Memory — Pick Up Where You Left Off

Every tutor in The Zion System™ outputs a session summary at the end of each lesson. It looks like this:

```
---SESSION SUMMARY---
Child: Zion, Age: 5
Topics covered: two-digit addition, carrying
Last problem: 47 + 38
Ready for next: three-digit addition
---END SUMMARY---
```

To pick up where you left off: **paste the summary at the start of the next session.** The tutor skips the intro and jumps straight to the next topic.
